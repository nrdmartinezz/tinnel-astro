## Infants and Toddlers: Gentle Beginnings for Healthy Teeth

Even before the first tooth appears, oral care is important. Gently wiping your baby’s gums with a soft, damp cloth after feedings helps remove bacteria and sugars that could affect developing teeth. Once the first tooth erupts—usually around six months—begin brushing twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and a tiny smear of fluoride toothpaste.

Avoid putting your child to bed with a bottle containing milk or juice, as this can contribute to baby bottle tooth decay. Water is the best choice if a bedtime drink is needed. We recommend scheduling your child’s first dental visit by their first birthday so we can monitor development and provide personalized guidance for healthy growth.

## Preschoolers (Ages 3–5): Building Routine and Responsibility

Preschoolers are eager to become independent, but they still need parental supervision when brushing and flossing. Encourage brushing twice a day with a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste and begin flossing once teeth begin touching.

Making oral hygiene fun with songs, colorful toothbrushes, and positive reinforcement can turn healthy habits into lifelong routines. Limiting sugary snacks and beverages while promoting a balanced diet also helps protect developing enamel.

Regular checkups at Harman-Tinnel Complete Family Dentistry allow us to identify potential problems early and provide professional cleanings to keep little smiles healthy.

## Elementary School (Ages 6–11): Encouraging Independence and Prevention

Elementary-aged children can begin managing their oral hygiene more independently, but occasional supervision and reminders remain important. Encourage brushing for two full minutes twice daily and flossing every day.

This stage is also ideal for preventive treatments such as dental sealants and fluoride applications. Sealants protect the chewing surfaces of molars where cavities commonly develop, while fluoride strengthens enamel and helps resist decay.

## Teens (Ages 12–18): Supporting Confidence and Healthy Habits

As children become teenagers, busy schedules and increased independence can sometimes make oral hygiene less of a priority. Continue encouraging consistent brushing, flossing, and healthy eating habits while maintaining regular dental visits.

Adolescence is also an excellent time to explore orthodontic options. At Harman-Tinnel Complete Family Dentistry, we proudly offer Invisalign® and fun **Wild Smiles braces**, giving kids and teens options that fit their personalities and lifestyles.

Cosmetic treatments such as professional teeth whitening may also be appropriate after oral health has been optimized, helping teens feel confident about their smiles during these important years.
