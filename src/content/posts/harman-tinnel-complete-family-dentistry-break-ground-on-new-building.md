## Building More Than Great Smiles

**New HTCFD Office Coming Soon**

We have some great news, we are moving! Yes, that’s right, HT Complete Family Dentistry is thrilled to share that we are moving to a brand new location. We are opening a new HTCFD office! We are proud to serve the communities of Overland Park, Lenexa, Overland Park, Leawood, and Kansas City and surrounding areas, and know this new build will allow us to expand our reach.

Our current office has allowed us to serve so many wonderful patients over the last 15 years, and we are so grateful for this office. We have outgrown the office that has served us so well all of these years. As we continue to gain more and more patients, we are confident that this new, larger office will comfortably serve our growing family.

The new building will showcase a modern exterior and impressive, spacious interior to match. Set right off of the highway, our new building will be easy to access, another way we can ensure your treatment and appointments go smoothly from start to finish. With a new large parking lot right outside the office, you will never have to search for parking again. 

All of our HTCFD patients know that our current office shares a lobby with other businesses in our building, but this will be a thing of the past in our new office. We have a private lobby dedicated to only HT Complete Family Dentistry patients! When you sit in our new, spacious lobby, you’ll notice that the office is over two times larger than our old office. We are growing from seven patient rooms, to 10 patient rooms as well, so you won’t have to sit in the waiting room for long.

The modern and roomy office may look different, but you will definitely recognize all of our prized state of the art technology. From our ergonomic patient chairs to our CBCT 3 D X-ray unit, our technology will be making the move with us. We plan to equip our new office with an additional crown technology unit, as well as a massage chair for our patients to relax in as they wait for their crown. We are also thrilled to announce the addition of a 3D printer in our new office! Our patients are our number one priority, so we are always searching for the top tier technology so we can provide each patient with the best experience and brightest smile!

We cannot wait to show you our new building. Our new office isn’t quite ready yet, but rest assured, our awesome construction team is working diligently and progressing through this new build as quickly as possible. Our anticipated opening date is early March, 2023. If you want to check out our new location in the meantime, the address is 7621 Frontage Road, Overland Park, KS 66204. That’s at the intersection of interstate I-35 and 75th in the parking lot of Floor and Decor. We are so excited for our new building, and we just know you will love it, too.

Make sure to check back here for more construction updates along the way. We are thrilled to share this new adventure with you and would love for you to follow along our journey to a new office. 

Are you interested in joining the HT Complete Family Dentistry Family? Don’t wait until our new office opens, call us to schedule your free consultation and join the HTCFD family today! We can’t wait to meet you.
