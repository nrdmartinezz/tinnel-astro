## What Are Dental Sealants and How Do They Work?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of your child’s back teeth—the molars and premolars. These teeth contain natural grooves and pits that can trap food particles and bacteria, making them especially vulnerable to cavities. Sealants act as a barrier, preventing plaque and debris from settling into these hard-to-clean areas.

Applied quickly and painlessly during a routine dental visit, sealants are a preventive treatment designed to stop decay before it begins. This simple procedure can help families avoid more extensive dental work in the future, making it an excellent investment in your child’s oral health.

## Why Are Sealants Especially Important for Children?

Children are particularly susceptible to cavities because their enamel is still developing and because brushing techniques may not yet be perfect. The deep grooves in newly erupted molars are difficult for toothbrush bristles to reach, allowing bacteria and food particles to accumulate.

Dental sealants provide an extra layer of protection during these important years. Research has consistently shown that children without sealants are significantly more likely to develop cavities in their permanent molars. By protecting these teeth early, we help establish the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

## When Should Your Child Receive Dental Sealants?

We generally recommend sealants when permanent molars first erupt—typically around age 6—and again when second molars appear around age 12. However, teenagers and even adults who are prone to cavities may benefit from sealants as well.

During your child’s routine checkups, our team evaluates their teeth and determines the ideal timing for treatment. Sealants are often combined with professional cleanings, fluoride treatments, and personalized oral hygiene instruction for maximum protection.

## How We Apply Sealants Safely and Comfortably

At our Overland Park office, comfort and trust are always priorities. Applying sealants is simple and completely painless. The process involves:

- Thoroughly cleaning and drying the tooth.
- Applying a gentle etching solution to improve bonding.
- Painting the liquid sealant onto the tooth surface.
- Using a special curing light to harden the material.

The entire process takes only a few minutes per tooth, requires no shots or drilling, and your child can return to normal eating and drinking immediately afterward. We monitor sealants during regular visits and can repair or replace them if necessary.
